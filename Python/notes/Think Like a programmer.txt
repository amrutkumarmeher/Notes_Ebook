................... THINK LIKE A PROGRAMMER ....................

Termology:
Constraint: A requirement or rule while solving a problem.

Lessons Learned:
-> the fox,the goose & the corn problem: Restarting the problem with different pov.
-> Sliding puzzle: It better to have a strategy & goal in mind than trial & error only.
-> Sudoku: try to solve variable with most constraint first.
-> The Quarrasi Lock: analyzing different forms(analogies) of a problem.

General Problem-Solving Techniques:
1. Always have a plan.
2. Restate the Problem.
3. Divide the Problem.
4. Start with What you know.
5. Reduce the Problem.
6. Look for Analogies.
7. Experiment.
8. Don't Get Frustrated.
